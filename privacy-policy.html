FairChance Finder

🔒 Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 12, 2026 · Last Updated: May 12, 2026

The short version: FairChance Finder only stores documents you choose to upload for job applications. Profile and application tracking data stays on your device unless you submit it directly to an employer site.

1. About FairChance Finder

FairChance Finder ("we," "us," or "our") is a free mobile web application designed to connect fair-chance job seekers — including individuals with criminal records — with employers who practice fair and second-chance hiring. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our app at fairchancefinder.com or any associated domains.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We are committed to your privacy. FairChance Finder does not:

  • Sell your name, email address, phone number, or any personal identifying information
  • Require a central account registration or login
  • Collect or store your criminal history, background information, or legal status
  • Share any data with third parties or advertisers
  • Track your location (though job listings are region-specific)
  • Use cookies for tracking purposes
  • Sell any data

3. Data Stored on Your Device

All information you enter into FairChance Finder is stored locally on your device only using your browser's localStorage. This includes:

  • Your profile name and city (if you choose to enter them)
  • Jobs you have applied to via the app
  • Your job preference settings (notifications, job type filters)

This data never leaves your device. It is not transmitted to our servers or any third party. You can clear all locally stored data at any time via the Profile page → "Clear All Data."

4. Uploaded Documents

If you upload a resume or document, that file is stored in Netlify Blobs so you can download it later and attach it to tracked applications. Uploads are limited to common document formats and are not sold or used for advertising.

Download links are generated for files associated with your browser. Do not share those links with anyone you do not want to access the file.

5. Job Listings

Job listings displayed in FairChance Finder are curated sample listings provided for informational purposes. When you tap "Apply Now," this records the application in your local device storage only. We do not submit any application on your behalf or transmit your information to employers.

Actual job applications must be completed directly with the employer through their official hiring process.

6. Third-Party Links and Resources

Our Resources page contains links to third-party organizations (e.g., legal aid, housing, workforce agencies). When you visit those websites, their own privacy policies apply. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external sites.

7. Service Worker and Offline Caching

FairChance Finder uses a service worker to enable offline functionality. This caches app files (HTML, CSS, icons) on your device so the app works without an internet connection. No personal data is included in these cached files.

8. Analytics

FairChance Finder does not currently use any analytics services. We do not track page views, user sessions, or any behavioral data.

9. Children's Privacy

FairChance Finder is intended for users 18 years of age and older. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted personal information through this app, please contact us immediately.

10. Security

The primary security consideration is the safety of data stored on your own device and documents you choose to upload. We recommend keeping your device secure with a PIN, password, or biometric lock.

11. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this Privacy Policy, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Your Rights

You can clear local profile and application data from the Profile page. If you need help with uploaded documents or have questions or concerns, please contact us below.
