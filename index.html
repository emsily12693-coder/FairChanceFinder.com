About FairChanceFinder

FairChanceFinder is a product of HomeIsYou LLC DBA FairChance Finder, based in Phoenix, Arizona. We exist to connect fair-chance employers in the Phoenix Valley with motivated job seekers who are ready for their second chance.

Our platform is 100% free for job seekers — always. Employers fund the platform at just $75 per 30-day job listing, ensuring candidates never have to pay a cent to find meaningful work.

We believe that everyone deserves the opportunity to prove themselves through their work, regardless of their background. Over 70 million Americans have a criminal record — that's 1 in 3 adults. Many of these individuals are skilled, motivated, and eager to contribute to their communities.

For questions or support, contact us at EPCSR@fairchancefinder.com.

Privacy Policy

What We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide: your name, email address, phone number, skills, background notes, and application data. All data is stored locally on your device using browser storage.

How We Use Your Data

Your information is used solely to connect you with fair-chance employers in the Phoenix Valley. We display your name, email, and phone number to employers only when you submit a job application.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties. Your data remains on your device and is shared only when you explicitly apply to a job.

Payment Security

Employer payments are processed securely by Stripe, a PCI-compliant payment platform. We do not store credit card numbers or financial details on our servers.

Data Retention

Your profile and application data is stored in your browser's local storage. You can delete your data at any time by clearing your browser data or logging out.
