💡 Know Your Rights

⭐ Arizona Fair Chance Hiring Law

Arizona HB 2617 limits when employers can ask about your criminal history. Many employers must wait until a conditional job offer before running a background check.

📋 Ban the Box

Arizona state agencies and many cities have removed the criminal history checkbox from initial job applications. You have the right to be evaluated on your qualifications first.

🛡️ What Employers Can & Can't Ask

Employers cannot use an arrest record that didn't result in a conviction to deny you a job in many circumstances. Older offenses may also be limited from consideration.
