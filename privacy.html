Privacy Notice

How FairChanceFinder handles information

This notice explains the job board data collected by the static site, application forms, resume builder, and upload function.

Information collected

Job search filters are processed in your browser. Resume builder content and saved application notes may be stored in browser local storage. Uploaded resume files are sent to the Netlify upload endpoint and stored in Netlify Blobs for later download by the same browser client ID.

How information is used

Information is used to provide job search, resume download, document upload, and application support features. Employer posting details are saved locally before redirecting to Stripe checkout.
